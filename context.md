> - **MarConnect is the wristband for children and pets** (a wristband for kids, a collar band for pets).

## 4. What is weak, and what a stronger company would fix

The product idea is serious. The writing currently explains components. A company at this level explains consequences.

| Today | Why it holds the brand back | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Features first (“digital fence”, “peer-to-peer”, “smartification”) | A shipowner, a captain, and a family member do not share one vocabulary | Lead with a person in the water. Then show the signal. Then name the product. |
| No story | Nothing to remember after the page | One signature film: the missing diver. |
| No “how it works” in plain steps | The bracelet is visible in the photo and never explained | Four beats anyone can repeat. |
| Safety and communication sit as separate blocks | They are one system: the person and the ship | Marpersonnel finds the person. MarNet carries the truth to the people who can act. |
| Privacy is a heading called “GDPR safe” | Sounds like a legal checkbox | Say the human version: the bracelet sleeps until a life is at stake. |
| One partner article from 2023 | No ongoing proof | A content engine: scenarios, explainers, partner news, deployment notes. |
| Typos and uneven English | “man-over-bord”, “informations”, “search and rescue operation” | A voice guide and a single editor. |
| No audience split | Fishing, shipping, and offshore buy for different reasons | Same product, three doors. |

## 5. Positioning

### The idea

**Life is the most valuable thing at sea.**  
Everything else — cargo, schedule, day rate, reputation — can be rebuilt. A person cannot.

MarConnect exists so that when someone leaves the ship, the ship already knows.

### The belief

**Precaution is better than cure.**

A search that starts after the shout is already late. The bracelet is on the wrist before the dive, before the watch, before the weather turns. It does not wait to be switched on in a panic. It wakes when the person needs it.

Cure is a helicopter, a search pattern, and a hope.  
Precaution is a quiet device that already knows where to look.

### The promise (proposed, qualitative only)

MarConnect gives a vessel a living picture of its people and a network that can carry that picture to the bridge, the ship, and the shore — in the moments that decide whether someone comes home.

Do not add minutes, metres, battery hours, or “guaranteed rescue” until those numbers are verified. Precision is a published word (“precise, real-time location”). Keep it. Do not invent a tolerance.

### Who it is for

| Audience | What they fear | What we say to them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shipowners and operators | A death, a stoppage, a claim, a headline | You already insure the hull. This watches the people. |
| Captains and bridge teams | Not knowing who is missing, or where | The alert arrives with a place, not only a shout. |
| QHSSE and crewing | Paper drills that fail in the water | The bracelet is the drill, worn every day. |
| Fishing and offshore crews | A person over the side in the dark | If you go in, the ship does not lose you. |
| Divers and anyone in the water | The surface being empty when you come up | The boat is already looking in the right piece of ocean. |
| Families | Silence | Someone on board can see them. |

### What we are not

- Not a consumer fitness band.
- Not a surveillance product that watches healthy people all day. Published behaviour is sleep-wake: transmit in an emergency.
- Not an autonomy company that has already automated the fleet. Autonomy is the horizon. Safety is the product today.
- Not a radiation company. The pictures show a **wireless signal**. Say signal, pulse, link. Never say the device “emits radiation” in customer-facing copy. The rings are a picture of contact, not a hazard.

## 6. Content management

The current site is a set of hard-coded sections. The next site should be a library. Marketing writes a block once. Design places it. No one retypes the diver story into three pages and lets the wording drift.

### Collections

| Collection | What goes in it | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Principles** | Short beliefs used everywhere | Life is the most valuable thing. Precaution is better than cure. |
| **Products** | Marpersonnel, MarNet, and later modules | Name, one sentence, plain-language steps, what it does not do |
| **Scenarios** | Films and stories | The diver. The night watch. The passenger. The engine room. |
| **Steps** | The reusable “how it works” | Wear. Wake. See. Reach. |
| **Proof** | Partners, quotes, deployments, press | Nornir, 2023. Empty slots for the next ones. |
| **Audiences** | A door for each buyer | Fishing, shipping, offshore, passengers, dive operations |
| **People** | Founders, board, spokespeople | Role verified, photo, one human sentence |
| **Articles** | News and explainers | Date, author, claim check |
| **Lines** | Approved headlines and buttons | See section 9 |
| **Legal** | Privacy, emergency-only transmission | Only approved wording |

### Fields every block should carry

- `id` — stable name, e.g. `scenario.diver`
- `status` — draft / approved / live
- `claim_level` — published / verified / proposed
- `audience` — who it is for
- `product` — Marpersonnel, MarNet, or both
- `line` — the sentence on screen
- `body` — the paragraph under it
- `visual` — what the picture must show
- `do_not_say` — banned overclaim for this block

### Writing rules

1. One human outcome in the first line. The product name can wait until the second.
2. Sentences a deckhand can read out loud. If a word needs a manual, it does not go in a headline. “Digital fence” can live in a specification. The headline is “The ship knows they are in the water.”
3. Present tense. Emergencies are not a brochure.
4. Concrete nouns: wrist, water, bridge, light, name, boat. Abstract nouns (“smartification”, “solutions”, “leverage”) stay out of the hero.
5. Two products, one sentence when they appear together: **Marpersonnel finds the person. MarNet tells the ship.**
6. Privacy is a feature of care, written in human language, with the legal line underneath.
7. Every scenario ends in an action: alert, location, rescue. Do not end on the technology.
8. British spelling is already on the site (“utilises”). Keep it: organisation, digitised, defence of the person, metres if a number is ever approved.
9. No fake customers, no fake sea trials, no stock-statistic (“every 11 minutes”). If we do not have the source, we tell the story without a number.
10. One editor owns the voice. New pages pull lines from this file. They do not invent a new slogan beside it.

### Voice

Calm. Certain. Warm. Short.  
Like a captain who does not raise his voice because the information is already good.

| Use | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| life, home, wrist, water, signal, bridge, wake, find | disrupt, leverage, next-gen, synergy, smartification |
| precaution, watch, alert, reach | guaranteed survival, military-grade (unless certified) |
| sleep until it matters | we track your crew all day |
| Norwegian engineering, made for hard water | startup, cheap, gadget |

### Page model for the next site (proposed)

Keep the four current pages. Give each one a job. Add scenario and product depth without turning the site into a manual.

1. **Home** — the belief, the diver film, four steps, two products, one proof line, one invitation.
2. **How it works** — the visual explanation. Signal rings. Bridge view. Sleep-wake. No jargon wall.
3. **Marpersonnel** — the person.
4. **MarNet** — the ship.
5. **Industries** — fishing, shipping, offshore. Same system, different first sentence.
6. **Stories** — the scenario library. Starts with the diver.
7. **About** — Norway, the transition from analog to digital, people, the standard we hold.
8. **Journal** — the old blog, renamed. News, explainers, partnerships.
9. **Contact** — a conversation, not a footer form.

The first build can ship Home, How it works, About, Journal, and Contact, with the other pages as approved copy waiting in this file.

## 7. The signature story

This is the story the brand should be known for. It is proposed creative, not a case study. Label it as a scenario until a real rescue can be told with permission.

### Title

**The diver who did not come up**

### The line over the film

Life is the most valuable thing.  
Precaution is better than cure.

### The story, as it should be told

A scuba diver drops off the stern on a quiet sea. The water is clear, then it is not. The shot is simple enough for anyone: a person, a blue that gets darker, a boat that gets smaller.

On the wrist is a Marpersonnel bracelet. It was put on before the dive. Nobody is thinking about it. That is the point. Precaution does not look like drama. It looks like a band on a wrist.

Something goes wrong. The diver does not surface on time. On the old ship, this is the moment the cure begins: a shout, a guess, a circle drawn on water that all looks the same.

On this ship, the bracelet has already woken. From the wrist, a signal spreads. On screen we draw it the way a person understands a voice in the dark: rings of light moving out through the water, up to the hull, onto MarNet, onto the bridge. Not a spreadsheet. A pulse. A place.

The bridge does not receive a mystery. It receives a person. A mark on the water. A direction.

The boat turns. The diver is found. The film does not celebrate the hardware. It shows two hands meeting at the surface.

End card:

**Life is the most valuable thing there is.**  
**Precaution is better than cure.**  
**MarConnect. Find them while it still matters.**

### Why this story

- A diver in open water is understood in one second. No one needs to know what a digital fence is.
- The bracelet is visible. The signal is visible. The rescue is visible.
- It teaches the product in order: worn in advance, silent until needed, then impossible to miss.
- It can be cut three ways: a six-second loop for the hero, a thirty-second film, a still campaign.

### The same truth in other scenes (later films)

Use the diver first. These are the next chapters, same four steps.

| Scenario | Picture | Lesson |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Night deck | A fisher in the dark, one boot on the rail, then the water | The ship sees the moment they leave the fence |
| Passenger | A traveller in light clothing, a wave, a wrist | Passengers are not trained. The bracelet still is. |
| Engine-room smoke | A figure, a corridor, a mark on a plan of the ship | Finding someone is not only an overboard problem |
| Offshore transfer | A basket, a swing, a gap of water | The dangerous minute is the minute between steel and steel |
| The quiet watch | A person on deck, healthy, bracelet dark | Most of the time, nothing is sent. Privacy is the default. |

## 8. How it works, in plain language

This is the explanation a visitor should be able to repeat. It is a translation of the published product, not a new specification.

**Marpersonnel is on the person. MarNet is on the ship.**

| Step | On screen | Words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Wear | A bracelet fastened before anyone enters the water | Precaution is a decision you make while everyone is still safe. |
| 2. Sleep | The device is dark. No trail of a person’s ordinary day. | It stays quiet. A life is not a data stream. |
| 3. Wake | The person is in the water, or the watch flags an emergency. Rings leave the wrist. | When it matters, it speaks. The ship hears a person, not a rumour. |
| 4. Reach | The bridge shows where they are. A boat turns. | Location is only useful if someone can act on it. MarNet carries it to the people who can. |

Published behaviours this must stay loyal to:

- Real-time location of personnel in an emergency.
- Automatic man-overboard detection and an alert to the crew.
- A digital fence and smart parameters (explain as “a line the ship already agreed is safe — cross it, and the alert starts”).
- Irregular health activity can raise an alert. Do not name a diagnosis, a heart-rate threshold, or a medical claim until a clinician and a certification say so.
- Sleep-wake transmission for privacy: data moves in an emergency.
- Search and rescue can be given the live location.
- MarNet: sensors, live data for decision makers, peer-to-peer links across machinery that was never digital, a foundation for later autonomy.

### The picture of the signal

Show the invisible part. That is the whole point of the design.

From the bracelet, draw clean rings of light. Cyan and white on deep water. The rings are not smoke, not lightning, not a danger symbol. They are contact. They travel:

1. from the wrist,
2. through the water,
3. to a node on the hull or the deck,
4. across the ship,
5. onto a calm screen on the bridge, where the person is a name and a point.

A child should get it: the person is lost, the watch calls the boat, the boat comes.

Put the same diagram in three frames so it never depends on the film alone:

- the ocean, wide, the person small, the rings large;
- the wrist, close, the device real;
- the bridge, quiet, one mark on blue.

Optional split screen, always: left, the person in the water; right, the same person as a point of light. The eye connects them without a paragraph.

## 9. Design inspiration

Aim: a maritime company that could sit beside the best industrial brands in the world. Cinematic, spare, expensive by restraint. The ocean is the set. The person is the subject. The product is small and exact.

### The feeling

Dark water. One human. One light that should not be there, coming from a wrist. Then a ship that answers.

Reference moods, not copies:

- A safety film shot like a feature, not like a catalogue.
- Product photography as calm as a watch campaign: metal, skin, salt, one highlight.
- Interfaces shown as a single truthful screen, never a dashboard collage.
- Norwegian coast as character: cold light, black rock, working boats, not a tropical postcard unless the story is specifically a warm-water dive.

### Colour

| Role | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Water and authority | The navy already on the site. Keep it. It is the brand’s dark. |
| Signal and action | The mint already on the button. Use it only for the pulse, the location, and the one action. If everything is mint, nothing is urgent. |
| Human | Skin, hi-vis yellow on deck, pale fabric in the water. Real colour. Do not grade the person into a cartoon. |
| Paper and bridge UI | Off-white, almost silent. Type in a serious grotesque. Large headlines, small labels. |

### The people in the water

Show the body in the ocean so the danger is obvious. This is the picture the current site never makes. The current hero is a man on deck, safe, in workwear. Keep that photograph — it is honest — and add the moment after he is not safe.

Cast and wardrobe, in this order:

1. **The diver.** Wetsuit or a simple dive outfit, mask pushed up, then under the surface. This is the hero film.
2. **The person in open water.** Light clothing or swimwear, clearly a human against a large sea, so a visitor understands “someone has fallen in” before they read a word. Shoot it with dignity. No glamour poses, no beach campaign, no body as decoration. They are cold, they are small, they are alive, and the signal is already leaving the wrist.
3. **The working crew.** Oilskins, hi-vis, boots, the bracelet looking like equipment rather than a gadget. This is who buys.
4. **The bridge.** One officer, one screen, low light, no panic acting. Competence is the luxury feeling.

Faces should be specific. Hands matter: fastening the bracelet, reaching, pulling someone onto a ladder. Salt on skin. Breath. The product is worn, so wrists are a campaign in themselves.

### Shot list for the first campaign

| # | Frame | What the viewer learns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Bow, open sea, no logo yet | This is about the water |
| 2 | Bracelet fastened on the dock | Precaution happens before the accident |
| 3 | Diver enters. Bubbles. The boat holds. | A normal working moment |
| 4 | They do not surface. The sea is blank. | Cure, started now, is a guess |
| 5 | Macro: the bracelet wakes. One mint point. | The device was already there |
| 6 | Rings expand across a wide ocean | A signal a non-engineer can read |
| 7 | Rings meet the hull and run through the ship | MarNet is the path |
| 8 | Bridge: a point, a direction | Someone can act |
| 9 | Boat turns. Ladder. Two hands. | The only ending that matters |
| 10 | Black water. The line. | Life is the most valuable thing |

### Motion

Slow until the wake. Then the rings are the only fast thing in the frame. The rescue is steady, not frantic. Premium brands do not shake the camera to fake urgency.

### What the site should not look like

- A template with three icon cards and a stock handshake.
- Neon “AI” graphics, hexagons, or a globe made of particles.
- Fake HUD clutter over the diver’s face.
- Smiling brochure crews who have never been cold.
- Red alarm chrome everywhere. Danger is the ocean. The brand stays calm.

### Art direction line

**A small light on a wrist. A large ocean. A ship that turns.**

## 12. Scenario library (copy-ready seeds)

Each one uses the same skeleton: ordinary minute, the loss, the wake, the signal, the reach, the line.

### The diver

A scuba diver leaves the boat and does not return to the surface on time. The bracelet was fastened on deck. It wakes. Rings of signal mark a place in deep water. The bridge sees it. The boat turns.  
**Line:** Precaution is better than cure.

### The watch

It is dark. A deckhand goes over the side and nobody is looking at that piece of rail. The fence notices before the next round of the watch.  
**Line:** The ship should not need a witness.

### The passenger

A guest is not trained for weather. The water takes them anyway. The same bracelet, the same wake, the same point on the bridge.  
**Line:** Safety cannot depend on experience.

### The health alert

Someone is still on board, and something about them is no longer ordinary. The system can notify the crew. We do not describe a disease, and we do not promise a diagnosis.  
**Line:** Some emergencies never reach the rail.

### The analog ship

The hull is old. The engines were never meant to speak. MarNet gives the vessel a way to carry live information between equipment, the bridge, and the shore.  
**Line:** You do not need a new ship to have a smarter one.
